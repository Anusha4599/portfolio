About

For three years I’ve worked on EV systems: Vehicle-to-Load, charging, DC-DC converters and AC power outlets.

I write the requirements, manage the suppliers, and take on the risks that sit between teams. My roots are technical: embedded systems, control theory and three IEEE papers. So I can go deep with engineers, then zoom out for stakeholders.

I decide with incomplete information, then test the decision against cost, timeline and quality together. At Mercedes-Benz that meant cutting hardware variants to lower cost, reusing proven software to save schedule, and closing a crash-zone safety risk. A cube that balances on its edge

A reaction-wheel self-balancing cube in the spirit of the Cubli, designed as a research platform for a future planetary explorer. A spinning wheel and a braking mechanism let the cube jump up and balance on one edge, using momentum transfer and an LQR controller.

I modelled and simulated the dynamics in Simulink and SimScape Multibody, derived the controller, and built the hardware on an Arduino Due with BLDC motor control, an IMU, an encoder and servo braking. The chassis was designed in Blender and AutoCAD, then laser cut.
